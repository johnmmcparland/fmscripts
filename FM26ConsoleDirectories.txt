New Backup Strategy Option
--------------------------
Create a folder outside of FM 26 Console in Documents
Copy each of the following
 
%LOCALAPPDATA%Low\Sports Interactive\Football Manager 26 Console 
to
%USERPROFILE%\Documents\Sports Interactive\FMBackups\Football Manager 26 Console\LocalLow\

and

C:\Users\%USERNAME%\AppData\Local\Sports Interactive\Football Manager 26 Console\Temporary
to 
%USERPROFILE%\Documents\Sports Interactive\FMBackups\Football Manager 26 Console\Local\

The rationale being it should let me backup everything that's needed, while keeping the main dir free
